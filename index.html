About Our Comic

Our comic is about the importance of taking care of our environment. It shows how improper waste disposal and pollution can harm nature and affect people's lives.

Through this story, we want to remind everyone that protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility. Simple actions like throwing garbage properly, recycling, and keeping our surroundings clean can make a big difference.
